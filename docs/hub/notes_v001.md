# ANATOMIX VOXINATOR 0.1

The first release. A vocal processor for the M-VAVE FM-1: a USB microphone goes in, and a corrected,
harmonised, formant-shifted and effected voice comes out of the pedal — while the screen shows an
animated cross-section of a human head and throat that moves with the voice and with every knob.

**Two engines.** MULTIVOICE keeps the original and adds up to three voices of its own: a third above, a
fifth above, an octave below, detuned, formant-shifted, placed left and right. GLIDE is the other half:
the detected pitch is pulled to the nearest note of the key and scale you chose, with the snap (RETUNE),
the slide (GLIDE), the human wobble (HUMANIZE) and the amount of the original pitch that survives the
correction (FLEX) under your hands.

**The anatomy is the interface.** The lungs fill with your level, the air moves through the trachea at
the speed of your breath, the vocal folds vibrate at the pitch you sing — and at the pitch they are
pulled to, when the correction is at work. The tract shortens and brightens with the formant shift, the
harmonies arrive as ghosted overlays of the same anatomy, the distortion makes the airflow jagged, the
delay leaves ghost outlines of the throat.

**Sixteen factory presets** (ROBOT BASTARD, TINY RAT, DEMON, ALIEN CHOIR, ROTTERDAM 1995, CHERUB,
TELEPHONE, HUGE HALL, UK GARAGE, NIGHTCLUB, EVIL TWIN, SYNTHETIC, LIQUID, RADIO MONSTER, BROKEN ROBOT,
CLEAN STUDIO) and sixteen of your own.

**A powered hub is required.** The FM-1's USB port drives no VBUS: it does not hand out power. A
bus-powered microphone will not work — plug the microphone into a powered hub and the hub into the FM-1.
44.1 kHz and 48 kHz, mono and stereo, 16-bit and 24-bit are all taken as they come.

**Not in this release:** the keyboard and the MIDI notes are not used yet, and the firmware has not been
on the hardware it was written for — this is the first build, and the silicon is still to be heard.
